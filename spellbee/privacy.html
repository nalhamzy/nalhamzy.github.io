SpellBee Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

SpellBee is designed as a local-first spelling practice app. Custom lists, practice stats, settings, and purchase status are stored on your device.

Information We Collect

SpellBee does not require an account. The app may use your device microphone only when you choose the spell-aloud practice mode. Speech recognition is handled by the device/platform services. SpellBee does not sell personal information.

Purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. We receive purchase status from the store so the app can unlock Premium features.

Ads

SpellBee may display ads outside the learning flow. Ads are provided by Google AdMob subject to Google's advertising and privacy policies.
