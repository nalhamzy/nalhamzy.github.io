Built for site closeout

JobProof is designed for contractors and field workers who need a clear local record before leaving the job site.

Capture locally

Create reports without an account. Capture, mark up, and generate PDFs offline after the app is installed.

Pair the work

Keep before and after photos together with notes, device time, GPS status, and accuracy when available.

Export when ready

Share a PDF report or a ZIP proof pack through your device. Report content stays on your device unless you export or share it.

Current app posture

The current release is intentionally simple and local-first.

  • No account: JobProof does not require login to create reports.
  • No ads: The current release does not include advertising.
  • No Firebase currently: The current release does not include Firebase analytics or crash reporting SDKs.
  • No voice or audio recording: Camera, location when allowed, photos, files, store purchase, and share flows are the relevant user-controlled capabilities.

Important limitation

Device-captured metadata is not legal notarization, not a legal verification service, not server time authority, and not a guarantee. GPS and timestamps reflect the device, permission state, and capture conditions at the time you use the app.
