Frequently asked questions

Does JobProof require an account?

No. The current release does not require an account to create reports.

Does JobProof work offline?

Yes for core capture, markup, PDF generation, and local export generation after install. Sharing through other apps may require the network access those apps need.

Where does my report content go?

Report content stays on your device unless you export or share it. The current release does not upload report titles, addresses, captions, photos, GPS coordinates, file names, or hashes to a developer server.

Which permissions does JobProof use?

JobProof uses camera access for capture, location access when you allow GPS metadata, photos and files for import or export flows, share flows for user-selected exports, and in-app purchase for the one-time Pro unlock.

Does JobProof include ads or tracking?

No. The current release has no ads, no external trackers, no Firebase analytics, and no Firebase crash reporting SDKs.

Can I restore a Pro purchase?

Use the restore purchase option in the app. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play, depending on where you installed JobProof.

Can support recover deleted local reports?

Support cannot recover report data that only existed on your device. Export or back up reports you need to keep.

Device-captured metadata is not legal notarization, not a legal verification service, not server time authority, and not a guarantee. GPS and timestamps reflect the device, permission state, and capture conditions at the time you use the app.
